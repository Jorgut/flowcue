## Description

Capture a thought without losing your focus. Reroute lives in your Mac menu bar and gives you a quick place to save ideas for later.

Open the capture window with Control–Option–Space, write or paste an idea, and save it locally. Use the inbox to search, edit, and archive your notes. When you are ready to continue in Codex, choose Copy and Open Codex, then paste the idea yourself.

Your notes stay on your Mac. Reroute has no account, cloud sync, advertising, or analytics.

Reroute is an independent third-party utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI. Codex is available separately.

## Review notes

This is a native macOS menu bar app. The capture panel opens on launch; Control–Option–Space toggles it. Command–Return saves a note and closes the panel. Open Inbox from the menu bar or the capture panel to inspect saved notes.

The Mac App Store build uses App Sandbox and does not launch a CLI or connect to the Codex App Server. Copy and Open Codex copies the selected note and opens the separately installed Codex application. If Codex is unavailable, the note remains saved and a message explains that it could not be opened.
